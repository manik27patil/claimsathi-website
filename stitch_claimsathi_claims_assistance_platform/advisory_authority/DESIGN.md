## Brand & Style

This design system expresses seasoned legal, financial, and insurance advocacy. Built to support policyholders navigating high-stakes claims, the aesthetic balances the institutional gravity of an elite advisory chamber with the warm, reassuring precision of personal advocacy.

The visual direction draws from **Corporate Modernism** infused with **Editorial Advisory Elegance**. It deliberately avoids cold bureaucratic detachment and frantic insurtech novelty. Instead, it cultivates an atmosphere of absolute reliability, meticulous attention to detail, and calm dignity. 

High-contrast typographic pairings—commanding serif headlines balanced by open, highly legible sans-serif data displays—project rigorous competence. Interfaces prioritize unhurried reading experiences, clear cognitive hierarchy, and reassuring visual anchors tailored for individuals and families dealing with critical financial milestones.

## Colors

The color architecture is built on institutional stability, purposeful resolution, and premium service accents:

- **Primary (`#10263F`)**: Deep Navy. Serves as the bedrock of visual gravity, applied to critical top-level navigational headers, primary display typography, and grounding structural surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#087F75`)**: Deep Teal Green. The color of resolution, positive forward movement, and affirmative action. Deployed for primary interactive buttons, success validation states, key milestone markers, and high-priority directional cues.
- **Tertiary (`#C8A96B`)**: Subtle Warm Gold. Reserved for concierge badges, premium status verification, trusted audit tags, and advisory milestones. It must never compete with actionable CTAs.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B`)**: Slate Charcoal. Used for core text content and high-legibility data tables, ensuring softened contrast compared to pure black.
- **Surface Foundation**: The base canvas is built upon Warm White (`#FAFBFC`) paired with quiet structural cards in Cool Stone (`#F3F6F8`) and hairline framing lines (`#E2E8F0`). Secondary descriptive copy rests in Slate Muted (`#64748B`).

Use light mode exclusively to convey transparency, clinical clarity, and document legibility.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the gravitas of **Merriweather** with the geometric legibility and open apertures of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Serifs for Headings**: Merriweather serves as an authoritative statement across displays and section headers. Its sturdy serifs ensure readability at display scales while establishing a cadence of trust and legal integrity.
- **Accessible Body Proportions**: Body copy utilizes Plus Jakarta Sans set with generous line heights (minimum 1.6x) to reduce visual friction for users reviewing intricate policy and legal jargon.
- **Numbers & Monetary Data**: Financial figures, policy reference codes, and status trackers must be rendered in medium or semibold weights of Plus Jakarta Sans with tabular lining numbers to guarantee vertical alignment in comparative fee calculations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored to a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to communicate order and steady progression. 

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and a maximum canvas container of `1280px`. Margins maintain an unhurried `3rem` boundary to cultivate an open, executive look.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Interactive touch targets preserve a minimum height of 48px to accommodate all demographics.

Rhythmic vertical spacing follows a dual scale: compact spacing (`space-xs` through `space-md`) governs input groupings, metadata rows, and badge pairings, while macro intervals (`space-xl` through `space-2xl`) frame major advisory stages, evidentiary sections, and form steps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids noisy, aggressive layering in favor of **Tonal Anchoring and Ambient Diffusion**:

- **Hairline Framing**: Structural surfaces primarily distinguish themselves via razor-thin borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Cards do not float detached from the page; they sit cleanly embedded within the layout architecture.
- **Ambient Card Shadows**: Where floating elevation is required (such as interactive case summary cards or active modal panels), shadows are tinted with the seed navy: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(16, 38, 63, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(16, 38, 63, 0.03)`.
- **Raised Focus Panels**: High-priority advisory summaries and active consultation workflows employ a slightly elevated state: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 38, 63, 0.08)`.
- **Tonal Layering**: Containers switch from the base `#FAFBFC` canvas to `#F3F6F8` internal blocks, establishing visual partitions without relying on high drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Roundedness Level 2** (`0.5rem` base, scaling to `1rem` on prominent cards and `1.5rem` on featured showcase containers).

This geometric choice strikes a balance: sharp zero-radii can appear intimidating or clinical, while heavy pill rounding feels overly playful. The 8px to 16px corner treatment introduces welcoming softness while maintaining a steady, structured stance for formal case files and financial statements. Small components (chips, inputs, regular buttons) utilize `0.5rem` (`8px`), while major structural cards leverage `1rem` (`16px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Teal Green `#087F75`)**: High-contrast, forward-moving CTA. Crisp white text, height 48px, horizontal padding `1.5rem`, rounded corner `0.5rem`. Hover triggers a rich deepening to `#06635B`.
- **Secondary Action (Deep Navy `#10263F`)**: For document retrieval, formal submission downloads, or account authentication. Deep navy background with crisp white typography.
- **Advisory Ghost / Outline**: `1px solid #E2E8F0`, foreground text `#10263F`. Hover state introduces `#F3F6F8` background tint with zero layout shift.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Concierge / Trust Badge**: Soft gold tinted background (`rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.12)`), text color `#936F2B`, hairline border in `#C8A96B`. Used strictly for SLA guarantees, verified advisor markers, and legal accreditations.
- **Status Indicators**: Soft pill structure using semantic fills with solid interior tracking dots (e.g., Active Review, Under Appeal, Rupee Recovery Complete).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` bordered by `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Corner radius `0.5rem`.
- Padding of `0.75rem 1rem` with `body-lg` text to guarantee effortless legibility for senior users.
- Focus state replaces the border with `#087F75` accompanied by an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.12)`). Label typography sits firmly outside the field in `label-md` `#1E293B`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 20x20px box with `0.25rem` radius for checkboxes; full circle for radios.
- Unselected: White canvas bordered by `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Selected: Solid fill of `#087F75` with crisp white SVG check icon.

### Cards & Advisory Dossiers
- Foundation card: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, border-radius `1rem`, internal padding `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- Header area separates with a subtle hairline rule, featuring a Merriweather `headline-sm` title paired with a status badge.

### Claim Milestones & Timelines
- Vertical linear track (`2px solid #E2E8F0`). Completed stages illuminated in `#087F75` with filled indicator nodes. Current stage highlighted by a pulsing dual-ring teal node. Future stages rendered in muted slate `#94A3B8`.
